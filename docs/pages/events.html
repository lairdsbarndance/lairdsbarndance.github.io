17th May 2025
Aldgate, London

Golden Wedding hoedown

Bluegrass and caller: Aldgate, London Joe Biursky caller, Luke Donovan guitar/singer, Joe Hymas mandolin, Tim Kent banjo/singer, Richard Partridge fiddle, Jolene Missing bass

“I just wanted to say thank you for the brilliant Ceilidh last night. I was amazed at the turnout and it was brilliant to see the huge range of nationalities present at the event. My wife and I had a great time.

Please pass on my congratulations to all those involved. I hope that this event becomes annual!”
